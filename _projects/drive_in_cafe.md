2100 sqft drive-in cafe

## Overview

Designed this drive-in cafe with one simple vision—to create more than just a place to eat, but a destination where people can pause, unwind, and make memories.

Every detail was carefully considered to balance aesthetics, comfort, and functionality. The warm earthy palette, natural textures, ambient lighting, and open layout create an inviting space that feels relaxed from the moment you arrive. Large glass facades blur the line between indoors and outdoors, while the thoughtfully planned seating caters to everything from quick coffee breaks to long conversations with friends and family.

The welcoming entrance, illuminated façade, cozy lounge seating, custom service counter, and nature-inspired accents all come together to give the café its own distinct identity. Every corner was designed to be visually engaging while ensuring a seamless customer experience and efficient staff movement.

Designing commercial spaces is about understanding how people interact with an environment. This project reflects my belief that a well-designed café should not only look beautiful—it should make people want to stay just a little longer.

**A space designed to welcome, connect, and create lasting experiences.**
