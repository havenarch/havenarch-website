An open industrial shell reworked as a calm interior: one long living volume, a precise kitchen wall, and a bedroom suite behind a timber screen.

## Narrative

The existing loft had height and light but little sequence. We kept the envelope and inserted a single piece of millwork that organizes cooking, storage, and the threshold to sleep. Soft acoustics and a muted palette let the original brick and steel remain the primary texture.

## Spatial sequence

The entry compresses under a lowered soffit, then the plan opens to the window wall. Dining sits in the brightest bay; a reading alcove occupies the quieter corner. The timber screen is both furniture and architecture — it holds the wardrobe, filters sound, and gives the bedroom a door without building a corridor.

## Material palette

- Existing brick, sealed and left honest
- Bleached ash cabinetry
- Honed limestone at wet zones
- Linen, wool, and low-sheen paint
