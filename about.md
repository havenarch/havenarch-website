<p>Established in 2024 by Principal Architect K.V. Swathi, Haven Arch Designs believes every space has the potential to become a sanctuary—an extension of the people who inhabit it. Based in Bengaluru, our studio creates architecture and interiors that feel like a gateway to your safe place.</p>

<p>We do not just design spaces—we craft experiences. Every project begins with a deep understanding of our client's vision, lifestyle, and aspirations. With a thoughtful blend of functionality, aesthetics, and emotion, we translate that vision into spaces that are personal, purposeful, and deeply livable.</p>

	</header>
	<div class="content">
