A hillside residence organized as a sequence of horizontal planes. Living volumes open to the horizon; private rooms step back into the slope.

## Narrative

The brief asked for a quiet house that could hold both daily family life and long views. We placed the main floor as a thin bar against the grade, with a sheltered courtyard on the uphill side and a continuous terrace downhill.

Structure is expressed as a regular bay of steel and timber. Infill walls are lime plaster and full-height glazing, so the plan reads as rooms of light rather than a collection of objects.

## Spatial sequence

Arrival is from a compressed motor court into a low, shaded gallery. The gallery releases into a double-aspect living room, then into the kitchen and dining wing. Bedrooms occupy the quieter eastern bar, each with a private outdoor room.

## Material palette

- Fair-faced concrete plinth
- White oak millwork and floors
- Bronze hardware and solar shading
- Native planting at the courtyard edge
